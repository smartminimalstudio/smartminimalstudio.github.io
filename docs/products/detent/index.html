05 / Edge keepers

Set once.
Slide daily.

Four small caps overlap the laptop’s upper edges, limiting upward and sideways movement. During setup, choose the smallest opening that clears your laptop, then insert and quarter-turn each keeper pin. The caps stay set when you open the front gates to slide the laptop in or out.

THE DETAILS THAT MAKE IT A SYSTEM.
07 /

One base.
Either side.

A 118 × 52 mm universal base works on either side. Nine indexed positions give each carriage 80 mm of travel, bringing the supports closer together when your setup needs it.

An optional link.

A telescopic rear bridge joins the rails and follows their width. Use matching angle, height and depth on both sides. It connects the supports; it does not add a desk fixing point.
