The short rail STL is 177.5 mm tall in its supplied orientation (48.5 × 14.1 × 177.5 mm), replacing the 227.5 mm standard rail. It reduces depth travel from 54 to 36 mm. Check every other part and your brim in the slicer; the standard 3MF is not an A1 mini plate layout.

What do I receive?

38 named STL models, five color-separated OrcaSlicer 3MF projects, FreeCAD and STEP files, English and Portuguese assembly PDFs, a hardware list and an offline assembly viewer. The standard support uses 44 printed pieces; optional parts are additional.

Which printer and material do I need?

The standard projects target a 260 × 260 × 260 mm build volume. Use PETG, a 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.20 mm layers. A shorter rail STL is included for the A1 mini, with less depth adjustment. Select your actual printer and filament profile before slicing; keep functional parts at 100% scale.

Will it fit my laptop and desk?

Fit is determined by the closed laptop dimensions, feet, vents, ports and the entire insertion path beneath your desk lip and crossbar. Indexed adjustment ranges below describe the mechanism, not a guaranteed laptop size range. Keeper openings are 18, 24, 30 or 36 mm: they must clear the shell and overlap its edges without squeezing. DETENT is not a port-specific dock and has no integrated USB connector.

What else do I need to assemble it?

Printed parts, screws, heat-set inserts and tools are not supplied. The hardware list below is for one complete pair; the rear bridge is optional. You will need calipers, suitable drilling tools, 2 mm and 2.5 mm hex keys and a heat-set insertion tool. Choose wood-screw length and pilot holes for your actual desktop thickness and material.

How long does it take to print?

Print time and filament consumption depend on your printer, profiles, infill, colors and optional parts. There is no measured production estimate yet. Slice the supplied projects with your actual settings for an estimate before starting a full kit. Fit-test coupons are included to check interfaces first.

Is this a finished, load-rated product?

D33 is a development edition. CAD geometry and sampled assembly paths have been checked; physical fit, click, load, impact, creep and fatigue qualification are pending. No load rating is declared. The images and films show the actual CAD geometry, not physical test footage. Test assembly and retention with a suitable surrogate before loading a laptop.
