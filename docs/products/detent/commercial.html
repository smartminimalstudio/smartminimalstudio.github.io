Before you make it.

Are the D33 files included?

No. This US$79 product is the commercial permission and downloadable licence terms. The D33 project package is a separate US$19 purchase. If you already own it, you only need the commercial licence to add the covered selling rights.

What happens after 12 months?

Renew with a separate purchase to keep manufacturing, advertising and selling. There is no automatic renewal. Arrange any remaining-stock sell-through with the studio in writing before expiry.

Is D33 a certified finished product?

D33 is a development edition. Physical fit, click, load, impact, creep and fatigue qualification are pending; no load rating is declared. A licence grants selling permission, not certification. Validate your process, installation and claims before supplying customers.

How is the licence identified?

The required licensee name, completed paid Payhip receipt and downloaded terms identify your grant. Keep all three. For a name correction or order help, contact the studio with your order reference.
